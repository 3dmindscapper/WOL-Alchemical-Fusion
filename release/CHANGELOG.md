# Changelog

## 1.1.2
- **Tomix, the Mixing Tome:** Tomi's scorch-marked cousin now does all the infusing; his pages shimmer through every element in turn. He floats across from Tomi in the Hub and your house (on the matching planter) (choose each arcana's version in his book) and comes to the arcana merchant during runs (infuse there). His journal (J, or pad Interact, with his book open) lists your infused versions and dual pairs page by page, per element. His book at home lists only arcana you've unlocked infused versions of, and reads as his ("Infusions", and per element how many arcana you've infused); Tomi's own book is back to the game's.
- **Conversations:** champions stay in place and you're paused while they talk; Confirm / Interact (pad) or Space / Enter / E / F finishes the line, then moves on. Lines still advance by themselves.
- **Fixed:** the Primordial Alchemist's true form could start during its intro (cycling colours and attacking while it talked); it now waits for the fight and its second phase. The true-form card lists the elements instead of blank squares.
- The infusion journal moved from the League board to Tomix.

## 1.1.1
The first update since 1.0.0; it includes 1.1.0 below.
- **The Primordial Alchemist's true form:** own every Primordial robe and the Alchemist gains a third phase, cycling through each Archon's colours, aura and arcana.
- **Alchemist's Journal:** a second page on the League board (Tab / Left / Right) listing every infused version and dual pair you've unlocked, per arcana, and the version Tomi uses.
- **Gear reactions:** rivals, Archons and the Alchemist comment on the robe you wear (Primordial, White Mantle, Grandmaster's) or your Grandmaster rank.
- **Bounties** are announced as centred cards: in the Hub, when the run starts, and at each step of progress.
- **LegendAPI is now required** (installed automatically); WoL Arcanos is recommended.
- **Nocturne** skips her 10-gem pitch when the shuffle is free and goes straight to the preset menu.
- **Team matches** use the biggest arena, the ally is truly on your side (no more friendly fire, the rivals target it), and all four wizards appear at once: each rival's arcana is shown, then everyone starts together.
- **Rival League board** at the Hub's wizard statue (Interact, or F2): rank, points, record per rival, the Alchemist, the White Mantle, the next bounty. The Hub's corner text is gone.
- **Final room on champion runs:** Sura no longer appears or speaks. The torches light, the champion arrives in a flash on the empty throne and introduces itself; Archons speak as their element, then fight with their own half-health and defeat lines.
- **The final room takes the champion's colours:** floor, walls, torches, the cosmic arena and the lights turn white for the Primordial Alchemist or the element's colours for an Archon. Sura and her health bar no longer flash on screen before the champion arrives.
- **Fixed:** beating a final champion now ends the run properly (the room waited forever for Sura's Chaos form).
- **The whole final castle** takes the champion's colours from the moment you arrive, not just the arena.
- **The arcana merchant** often sells arcana you've unlocked infused versions of as one of those versions (config Infusion.UnlockedStockChance, 50%).
- **Fixed:** robe icons in rival loadouts show the robe in its colours instead of a solid block; the Haunting ghost, Hasted after-images and the Vengeful soul no longer draw as red boxes.
- **Primordial robes:** beating an Archon unlocks its element's robe in the wardrobe: the Archon's look and aura, and that element's arcana hit harder, cool down faster and inflict their status more often (numbers differ per element).
- **Grandmaster's Robe:** reach the league's top rank for a gold robe that deals +30% damage to wizards.
- **Alternate endings:** after an Archon or the Primordial Alchemist, its last words and an ending of its own; Sura keeps the standard ending.
- **Elemental ghouls, blobs and golems** (and elemental Coffin Knights / Cyclops) throw a burst of their element when they hit you: sparks, embers, droplets, shards, dust, gusts.
- **Council portraits** on the loading board are recoloured in their element or variant (no more greyed-out tint).
- **Polish:** NPCs, shop wares and pickups keep their colours in the recoloured castle; no bounty card on the title screen; in team matches the rivals fight your ally too; one menu at a time in the Hub.
- **Loading board:** the final circle shows a white Sura for the Primordial Alchemist and an element-coloured one for an Archon.
- **Archons** look like primordial versions of their element: the whole body in the element's colours, with a permanent aura.
- **Portal labels:** every portal the mod opens says what's behind it (rival and difficulty, tournament, team match, Echo, rematch, trial, way back).
- **Fixes:** the team match ally now attacks the rivals; Tomi's book in your house lists all your arcana again (the house was treated as a run).
- **Test hotkeys:** Alt+1 / Alt+2 tournament / team portals, Alt+3 Primordial Alchemist final, Alt+4 Archon final, Alt+5 Archon portal, Alt+6 instakill. With the hotkeys on, nothing is unlocked for good.
- **The Primordial Alchemist** (formerly the White Alchemist): a godlike manifestation of the essence every element flows from. It now introduces itself before the fight, comments on your infused or dual-infused arcana and on the element you lean on, and speaks at half health and in defeat.

## 1.1.0 (not released on its own; part of 1.1.1)
- **Nocturne the Cardist:** her shuffle is free, and talking to her opens a preset menu:
  - full shuffle
  - arcana only
  - robe and relic
  - element build
  - alchemist (lands on your unlocked infused and dual versions)
- **The White Alchemist** (the Primordial Alchemist from 1.1.1): a new final champion.
  - A pure-white wizard with a permanent aura, every arcana dual-infused, and a Transmuted second phase.
  - Beating it teaches a new infused version; the first win gives the White Mantle, a wardrobe robe via LegendAPI (white look and aura, infused arcana hit harder and cool down faster). Without LegendAPI it's worn from the modifiers menu.
- **Rival League:** named rivals per element who remember your record, with league points, a rank shown in the Hub, and gold bonuses.
- **Rival portals** can hold a three-round tournament or a 2v2 team match with an allied wizard.
- **Bestiary:** five new special mini-bosses, for eleven in all: Golem Gauntlet, Turret Fortress, Summoner Coven, Lancer Phalanx, Archer Volley.

- Elemental giants on elemental stages, with borrowed skills, player arcana, intros and boss bars.
- Six special mini-bosses: Giant Cyclops, Mimic King, Cyclops Eye Court, Elemental Conclave, Hive Mother and Gravewarden.
- Elemental Coffin Knights, Cyclops, blobs and ghouls.
- Twelve elite traits, with gold scaled by risk.
- Rival wizards in starting-room portals:
  - robes and relics, cursed relics included
  - difficulty that scales with the run
  - the Echo of your last run
  - rematches
  - Archons
  - voice lines
- Chaos stages; Radiant and Prism stages with WoL Arcanos.
- Corrupted and infused Council members.
- Final champions in place of Sura.
- Arcana infusion at Tomi:
  - shelf sacrifices
  - dual infusion
  - permanent unlocks, with original-element pairs
  - random run versions
  - Nocturne's shuffle support
  - pre-infused drops
- Infusion trials, bounties and an in-game modifiers menu.
